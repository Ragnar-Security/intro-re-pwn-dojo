A guard program is holding a secret 64-bit number. It repeats whatever you type back to you, and it also asks you to guess the secret. The guard is careless about *how* it repeats your words, and that mistake lets you read the secret out of the program's own memory.

Connect, read the program's prompts, and find a way to make it reveal the number. Give the right secret and it prints the flag. The secret is new every time the program starts, so you cannot look it up in advance.
